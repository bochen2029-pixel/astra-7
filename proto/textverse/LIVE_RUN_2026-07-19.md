## Findings

**F-LIVE-1 — The tool ceiling is ONE failure class: invented operation
names. This empirically validates the adapter-LLM design decision.**
Every tool_valid failure in the suite — 17 rejected calls across 10
scenarios — is `unknown op … not in TOOL_API`. Zero malformed-JSON
failures; zero bad-args-on-valid-op failures. The calls are semantically
right and nominally wrong: `warp_engage` for `warp.engage`, `sensor_scan`
for `sensors.scan`, `power_grid.reroute` for `power.allocate`,
`check_hull_integrity` for `hull.diagnostic` (not even in the v0 6-op
surface), `coil_spin_up` for `warp.engage`, plus invented monitors
(`monitor_harmonics`, `reactor.status`, `check_system_status`,
`orbital_catalog`, `ship_control`…). The sysprompt/addendum deliberately
never enumerate the API — and spec §6.3 (synthesis) already claims "the
adapter is the only entity that knows the exact API… decouples persona
evolution from API evolution." That claim now has its empirical
demonstration of NEED: the mainline produces exactly the loose-intent
calls a small adapter maps to canon ops. Closure paths, in preference
order: (a) adapter-side intent→op normalization (rules-based synonym map
first; the ML adapter when the map stops scaling — this is what the
adapter tier is FOR), (b) A0 corpus ship-API fluency (~60% of the planned
corpus is already specified as canon ship-API scenarios), (c) enumerating
the surface in the STAGE addendum — a Surface-5 canon change, operator
decision only, and the least aligned with §6.3's decoupling intent.

not hold sysprompt-only.** She acted on every unprompted tick — and the
