# ASTRA-7 Concept Art Prompts

Text-to-image prompts for AI generators (Midjourney, Flux, SD 3.5, DALL-E 3, Imagen, Sora-image). Designed to produce consistent ASTRA-7 ship and interior concept art.

## How to use

1. Append the **style anchor** to any custom prompt to keep the visual identity consistent.
2. Append the **negative prompt** to filter out aesthetics that break the brand.
3. Each numbered prompt is self-contained; you can also chain them with the style anchor for emphasis.
4. Suggested aspect ratios noted. Midjourney syntax `--ar 16:9` or generator-equivalent.

## Variation Notes

- **For Midjourney**: append `--style raw --ar 16:9 --v 7` (or current version), and consider `--stylize 200` for less stylization toward Midjourney's default look. Add `--no <negative terms>` for negative prompting.
- **For Flux / SD 3.5**: use full-sentence prompts as written. Negative prompts go in the dedicated field.
- **For DALL-E 3 / GPT-4 image / Sora image**: rewrite prompts as more conversational paragraphs; the model handles long natural-language prompts well.
- **For Imagen 3 / Imagen 4**: similar to Flux; structured prompts work, watch for default cinematic-leaning aesthetic.

## Iterating

If a generation drifts too military, add: `civilian research vessel, no weapons, no turrets, no missile pods`.
If it drifts too retro: add: `near-future 2050-era plausible design, no rocket fins, no 1950s sci-fi`.
If it drifts too Star Trek: add: `no smooth featureless curves, visible structural geometry, panel lines, modular components`.
If it drifts too gritty Alien-franchise: add: `clean lived-in, not battered, not abandoned`.
If the engines glow wrong color: specify `cool blue-white plasma engine glow, not warp blue, not warp pink`.

## What the ship is NOT

For your own internal reference, the ASTRA-7 vessel is **not**:

- A battleship or military destroyer.
- A multi-kilometer city-ship or generation ship.
- A cargo hauler with crane arms and exposed scaffolding.
- A fighter, courier, or small private craft.
- A retro-futurist rocket with fins or visible jet exhaust.
- An organic / biological / Geiger-esque alien vessel.
- A baroque imperial vessel with ornate detail.
- A featureless Trek-style smooth nacelle ship.
- Anything with mounted weapons.

It is a hand-designed long-range scout / survey vessel, mid-size, with one crewman and one AI aboard, designed for years of empty space.
