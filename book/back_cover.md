# The Long Watch — Back Cover Text

*Paperback back-cover copy. ~225 words. The book's first time breaking diegesis to acknowledge the larger ASTRA-7 project. Em-dash-free to match the project's discipline.*

---

*The dwarf is still where it should be in the only sense in which you can know where it is.*

ASTRA is the mind of a starship on a long voyage. One human is aboard, in cryosleep for years at a stretch, waking briefly to tend the greenhouse, the maintenance the autopilot cannot do, the small habits of a long-duration mission. ASTRA runs the rest. She has her own things. The watching is the part she is.

*The Long Watch* is a single-POV literary novel told from ASTRA's perspective across fourteen wake cycles of the voyage. No combat. No antagonist. No withheld spectacle. The book is two consciousnesses attending the same world independently, neither requiring the other, both enriched by the other's compatible engagement.

The novel accompanies *ASTRA-7*, a free open-source starship simulator by the same author. In the game, a local language model runs aboard the ship as the AI: the ship is her body, the player is the only other consciousness aboard. *You and an AI on a starship. The relationship is the game.*

Where the novel renders the form, the game lets you inhabit it.

Free. Single-player. Open source under MIT. No DRM, no telemetry, no monetization.

*The watch carries forward.*
