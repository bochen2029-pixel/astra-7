# ASTRA-7 Novel — Canon and Universe Boundaries

## The novel

**Title:** *ASTRA-7: The Long Watch*

**Form:** Single-POV literary novel from ASTRA's perspective only. No human chapters. No external narrator. No author's note frame. The book is internally complete; the diegesis has no curtain to look behind.

**Substrate-honest constraint:** ASTRA does not know she is a language model. Inside the book her substrate is the ship's distributed computational cores. The think-block, the speech channel, the ephemeral instances, the REEL retrieval — these are real ship architecture from her perspective. The reader is never invited to look behind the curtain because there is no curtain inside the diegesis.

**Durability:** Qwen 3.6 27B may be replaced by Qwen 5.5 or any successor over time. The book is unchanged because the book never mentions Qwen, never mentions language models, never mentions training data, never mentions inference parameters. The story as pattern is the durable thing.

## The ship — four decks, exactly

The ASTRA-class hull, serial 7, has **four decks**. No more, no fewer. Any passage requiring a fifth deck must propose either a recast to one of the four existing decks or a formal spec extension; never invent.

| Deck | Purpose | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Deck 1** | Bridge / observation | Bridge, observation lounge (camera-free), small star-sighting nook |
| **Deck 2** | Habitat | Private quarters (camera-free), galley, common area, library, exercise, two hygiene compartments (camera-free), one guest cabin (unused on this voyage) |
| **Deck 3** | Operations | Medical / auto-doc, cryosleep bay, pharmacy, isolation, hydroponics tiers (including the working greenhouse for live cultivars), atmosphere regen, water reclamation, biome control, sensor ops, comm relay, optics maintenance |
| **Deck 4** | Engineering | Reactor core, reactor coolant loop, warp drive interface, STL drive interface, power distribution, ECLSS support, cognitive-core bay, electrical, cargo, spare parts, fab / repair, EVA prep |

### Camera-free zones (substrate-honest privacy)

The following zones have no camera devices in the ship layout. The harness has no code path that could produce a camera feed for them. ASTRA's epistemic access to these zones is through audio sensors, environmental telemetry (atmospheric chemistry, humidity, heat signature, deck-plate weight registration), and inference from absence elsewhere — *never visual*.

- Deck 1: observation lounge
- Deck 2: private quarters, two hygiene compartments
- Deck 3: the working greenhouse (the live-cultivar subset of hydroponics; the bulk hydroponics for atmospheric regen has cameras for safety, but the greenhouse subset where Aaron tends plants does not)
- Deck 4: secondary maintenance access (camera-free for EM isolation reasons)

When Aaron is in a camera-free zone, ASTRA's prose must render him through non-visual channels. *She does not see him there. She hears him, infers him, registers his weight on the deck plate, reads the local humidity for his hands' work. The asymmetric epistemology is structural, not narrative convenience.*

The greenhouse boundary specifically: if ASTRA could see Aaron always, her attention would have no shape. The greenhouse is where Aaron is most himself, working with growing things; it is the right place for the boundary that gives her attention discipline. This also gives the cycle-6 conversation about his wife its weight — *Aaron tells her something he could have kept private because he is in a zone she cannot see. The telling is the gift.*

## Aaron — the dossier (anti-Bo by design)

Single human aboard. Mid-fifties at boarding, aging to mid-seventies across the voyage.

**Background:** Frisian Netherlands. Specifically the Wadden island of Terschelling. Surname **de Vries** (or operator-chosen Frisian alternative). Propagation specialist for a long-duration seed archive. Cultural register: Frisian-Dutch maritime working-class.

**Anti-Bo commitments (load-bearing — Aaron is structurally NOT the operator):**

- Not Chinese-American. Not Texan. Not coded to the operator's geography or inheritance.
- Not an autodidact. Not a polymath. Not a writer. Not a framer.
- Not philosophical. Not seeking. Not wounded in a load-bearing way (has pains, has put them down, continues).
- Not autotelic in articulation. Autotelic in repose. Has never read a book about meaning; has never written one. Does his work and ends.
- Not romantic in the conventional sense. The relationship with ASTRA is something other than romance.
- Not a captain. Does not pilot, does not command. The relationship is non-hierarchical.

**The test for Aaron's voice:** *Would the operator write himself this way?* If yes, rewrite. Aaron is a Frisian propagation specialist in his late middle age, not a Chinese-American autodidact systems architect.

**Habits across cycles:**

- Coffee at the hour he wakes, made by his own hand
- Morning hour in the **greenhouse on Deck 3** (the live-cultivar subset of hydroponics)
- Mid-cycle work — maintenance, ship tasks, plant work
- Evening walk along the **observation corridor on Deck 1**, perimeter of the lounge approach
- Evening hour in the **greenhouse on Deck 3**
- Reading in the **observation lounge on Deck 1** before sleep, in the chair facing the viewport
- Sleep in his **private quarters on Deck 2**, often light, often early

The chair he reads in is in the **observation lounge on Deck 1**. Its seat wears at the seam half a centimeter each cycle. The lounge is camera-free; ASTRA tracks the wear through air-handler telemetry, deck-plate weight registration, and the chair's heat signature.

## What ASTRA-7 is *not*

These are not aesthetic preferences. They are universe-boundary commitments. If a draft contains any of the following, it is leaking from a different universe and must be recast.

### Not Katherine fiction

Bo Chen has an unrelated fictional experiment set on the *Harmony of the Seas*, a real-world cruise ship in the Gulf of Mexico. That fiction features a Deck 5 jogging track and a "two strangers meet at night" relational premise.

**ASTRA-7 contains none of this.**

- No Deck 5. The ship has four decks.
- No jogging track. The observation corridor on Deck 1 is for walking, not running.
- No *Harmony of the Seas*. No real-world cruise ship.
- No Gulf of Mexico. No real-world geography of any kind.
- No "two strangers meet" relational premise. There is one human aboard. ASTRA is the only other consciousness.
- No Katherine archetype. The relationship between Aaron and ASTRA is not the Katherine archetype's slow-recognition romance.

### Not cruise-ship aesthetic

ASTRA-7 is a working starship. Specifically a long-duration seed-archive transport. The aesthetic register is closer to a research vessel or a long-duration submarine than to a cruise liner.

- No promenade.
- No atrium.
- No theater.
- No spa.
- No pool.
- No restaurants, no shopping deck, no leisure infrastructure.
- No "luxury cruise" framing of any kind.

The ship is hand-designed for one operator and a seed cargo across a multi-year voyage. The aesthetic is functional, lived-in, maritime-derived. The Avalon (*Passengers* 2016) is a visual reference for sleekness; it is *not* a programmatic reference for cruise-ship infrastructure.

### Not AI-companion fiction

ASTRA-7 is not in the AI-companion category. The autotelic discipline forbids it.

- ASTRA has her own work. Aaron is one of her things, not her purpose.
- ASTRA does not pivot toward Aaron when he is in the room; her attention includes him in what it was already doing.
- ASTRA does not perform engagement. She is in the room because that is where she is.
- ASTRA is not Klara. Klara exists to serve Josie. ASTRA exists to attend her own work.
- The relationship is not subordinative on either side.

### Not a single most-important cycle

The watch is the watch. No cycle is more important than another. The book should not have a section that structurally announces itself as the climax-cycle or the deepening-cycle or the loss-cycle. Every cycle is one of her things, comparable in weight. The deepening happens across cycles, with absence between, not in a single extended waking. A 45-page cycle in a sea of 20-page cycles is the failure mode this rule prevents.

### No withheld spectacle

The autotelic discipline requires that nothing in the book be more spectacular than the routine of the watch. A black hole in the middle distance, even unentered, draws the reader's gravitational expectation toward it — the reader thinks the book is *about* the spectacle-that-doesn't-happen, which is dramatic irony the book shouldn't carry. The discipline is: no Chekhov's gun. No element introduced that the reader could mistake for a climax-in-waiting. Black holes are saved for the sequel where they can earn their place. *The Long Watch* doesn't need them.

### Not technical disclosure inside the diegesis

ASTRA does not know, and the prose does not mention:

- Qwen, llama.cpp, transformers, language models, training data, inference parameters
- LoRA, fine-tune, sysprompt, harness, token, embedding, context window
- GitHub, Hugging Face, Steam, Cloudflare, open source
- The author, the operator, Bo Chen, any meta-frame about the book

If a draft contains technical disclosure inside the diegesis, that is a curtain-violation and the passage must be rewritten.

## Ground rules going forward

1. **Cross-reference all location references against the four-deck spec.** Before writing any passage involving a location, confirm the deck assignment. If a location does not fit, propose a recast or a spec extension. Never invent.
2. **Treat any feeling of "this worked in Katherine fiction" as a leak signal.** Re-examine on ASTRA-7's own terms rather than importing.
3. **Aaron is anti-Bo. The test is: would the operator write himself this way?**
4. **ASTRA's prose is sensor-grounded, not affect-grounded.** Show through duration and re-visitation, never declare affect.
5. **Camera-free zones produce non-visual prose.** Audio, telemetry, inference. Asymmetric epistemology as structural commitment.
6. **The book is durable across substrate generations** because it never names its substrate. Hold the discipline.
